About

I move between three worlds that most people keep separate. Give me a network to configure and I'll trace every cable and IP until it's stable. Give me a blank canvas and I'll bring a designer's eye for color, spacing, and hierarchy. Give me a browser and I'll turn that design into a real, interactive frontend.

Lately, a lot of that build process happens through vibe coding — working conversationally with AI tools to prototype, iterate, and ship interfaces at a pace that traditional workflows can't match. I treat it as a serious craft: fast iteration, real judgment on what to keep.
